## Objective of the meeting
Rehearsal of the Milestone 3 presentation with the project advisors, followed by their feedback on slide content, visual communication, and review of key privacy and application requirements.

## Agenda
1. Milestone 3 presentation rehearsal.
2. Feedback on presentation structure and slide content.
3. Alignment on privacy, licensing, accessibility, and AI disclaimer implementation.

## Decisions taken

- **Presentation & Slides:**
  - **Context Slide:** Add an illustrative image/diagram to the project context slide to visually support the problem statement and provide clear background context.
  - **Data Anonymization:** Explicitly specify how code and repository anonymization is handled, detailing in which cases data will or won't be anonymized.
  - **License Table:** Simplify or provide a more specific breakdown of the licenses table to ensure transparency and clarity regarding third-party frameworks and tool licensing (e.g., LINDDUN).
  - **Accessibility:** Highlight during the accessibility discussion that a formal accessibility audit/analysis was conducted for the application interface.

- **Application & Development:**
  - **AI Reliability Notice:** Add an explicit in-app disclaimer stating that AI-generated responses and suggestions may contain errors, reminding users to verify recommendations.
  - **Right to be Forgotten ("Forget Data"):** Implement the "forget data" functionality in the platform, ensuring users can request and perform complete deletion of their stored data and analysis history.
